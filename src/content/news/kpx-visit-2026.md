startDate: 2026-11-13
location: "Indiana University Bloomington"

description:
  - "Representatives from Korea Power Exchange (KPX), South Korea, will visit the EML Lab on November 13, 2026. The visitors are Minwoo Jang and Yongsik Kim of the Supply and Demand Forecasting Team, Energy Planning Department. KPX operates South Korea’s electricity market and power system and plays a central role in national electricity supply-and-demand planning."
  - "The visit will focus on electricity demand forecasting, particularly predicting large new electricity loads and managing long-term uncertainty. KPX initiated the visit to seek EML’s perspectives on forecasting methodologies, data requirements, and ongoing research."
  - "EML Research Director Joon Y. Park and his research collaborators in Korea, including EML collaborator Yongok Choi, have developed core electricity-demand forecasting models for KPX for more than 30 years. This longstanding relationship provides a foundation for continued research exchange and collaboration."
